> English summary: Comparative research behind amendment 19 of the legal annex. North-Carolina-style open voter files with individual vote history are rejected; the proposal instead opens aggregates, receipt-free personal checks, copy-free in-person inspection, minimised and traceable candidate copies, no-export audit and research access, and court access, while keeping who voted closed to everyone but the voter and the court.
