# Public access to statewide voter registration lists: US South (AL, AR, FL, GA, KY, LA, MS, NC, OK, SC, TN, TX, VA, WV)

Research date: 2026-11-12. Items dated before 2024 are marked **[pre-2024, possibly outdated]**. "Not found" means no reliable source turned up; nothing was filled in by guess.

**Note on the cross-state compilation.** NCSL's 50-state page "Access to and Use of Voter Registration Lists" returned HTTP 403 and could not be read. The only cross-state compilation read in full was the EAC's undated "Availability of Voter File Information" (https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf), which cites NCSL, the US Elections Project and state officials. It has no date, and its prices and fields predate several 2024–2026 changes. Treat it as **[undated, likely pre-2024, possibly outdated]**. State primary sources were used wherever they could be reached. The pages for FL DOS, GA SOS, VA ELECT, Courthouse News and the Washington Times returned 403. For those, facts come from search-result summaries and are flagged as such.

## Q1. Who may obtain the statewide list, and through what channel (free download, sale, request)?

### Takeaway
Three groups emerge. (1) **Anyone may get it**: NC (free weekly download with no sign-up), MS (free weekly emailed link to anyone who signs up, since 2025), FL (free monthly extract on request), and by sale or request in AL, AR, GA, LA, TX and WV. (2) **Restricted to in-state people**: SC (registered SC voters only), OK (since 1 Nov 2025, OK-resident US citizens, candidates and parties, free), and TN (anyone certifying "political purposes"; a residents-only rule appears only in a county-level search summary). (3) **Restricted to political or civic users**: VA (candidates, parties, PACs, officeholders, voter-participation nonprofits) and KY (candidates, parties and committees, plus others at the Board's discretion, for non-commercial purposes; foreign requesters barred). Only NC offers a fully open, no-account, free public bulk download of the whole statewide file.

### Cited Findings
- **NC.** The State Board posts a statewide ZIP and 100 county ZIPs of the voter registration file, described as "the most up-to-date publicly available information." They are updated weekly on Sunday evenings and have no stated eligibility rule or fee. — [NCSBE Voter Registration Data](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-registration-data). The EAC compilation lists NC as "Open, $0." — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **NC (statute).** County boards "shall" provide lists of registered voters "upon request" for actual cost. Each party chair gets one free list in specified years. — [G.S. 163-82.10(c),(d)](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10.html)
- **MS.** Since the 2025 "Statewide Voter File Weekly Distribution Program," the SOS sends a statewide voter file and a statewide voter history file every week, "at no cost," to "those who sign-up," via a Sharefile link. It is "the same type of file provided when a statewide voter file is requested through a public records request." Requests outside the weekly distribution go through the public records process. — [MS SOS program PDF (2025)](https://www.sos.ms.gov/content/documents/about_us/2025%20Voter%20File%20Weekly%20Distribution%20Program.pdf)
- **FL.** The Division of Elections generates a "publicly available voter extract file" every month. A request must arrive at least 24 hours before the scheduled availability date. An older version of the page said there is "no fee associated with this complimentary disk." — [FL DOS Voter Extract Disk Request](https://dos.fl.gov/elections/data-statistics/voter-registration-statistics/voter-extract-disk-request/) (search summary; page returned 403). News reports say the data "can be obtained by anyone who asks, free of charge." — [News4Jax, Oct 2020](https://news4jax.com/news/2020/10/21/registered-to-vote-in-florida-some-of-your-data-is-public) **[pre-2024, possibly outdated]**
- **AL.** HB67 (2026 Regular Session, enrolled) provides that voter lists and 10 election cycles of history "shall be accessible to any person who files an application to receive an electronic copy of a voter list and pays the required fee." It is effective 1 June 2026. It passed the House on 5 Feb 2026 and the Senate on 9 Apr 2026. The governor's signature or Act number was not verified. — [AL HB67 enrolled](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB67-enr.pdf). The SOS says the statewide list is "available for purchase online pursuant to Alabama Code Section 17-4-38." — [AL SOS](https://www.sos.alabama.gov/node/3271) (search summary)
- **AR.** The SOS provides three statewide files: VR (registration), VH (vote history) and a combined VRVH. Requests are made on a Data Request Form with payment. — [AR SOS Data Request Form](https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/elections/Data%20Request%20Form.pdf) (search summary; the "Updated" version returned 404)
- **GA.** The SOS sells statewide and district lists under O.C.G.A. § 21-2-225, separately from the Open Records Act. No eligibility limit is stated. — [UGA First Amendment Clinic guide, May 2024](https://firstamendment.law.uga.edu/work/guide-to-accessing-election-records-in-georgia/)
- **LA.** Act 425 of 2018 (HB 211) amended R.S. 18:154 so that a registrar may permit copying of a list "upon the request of a single person of the age of majority." The earlier multi-voter written-request requirement was repealed. — [LA Legislature doc](https://legis.la.gov/Legis/ViewDocument.aspx?d=1085729) (search summary). The Department of State sells lists through ERIN, on GeauxVote or at registrar offices. — [LAC 31:II.105 mirror](https://d1tvxue8o4w0fl.cloudfront.net/regulation/louisiana-administrative-code/title-31-elections/part-ii-voter-registration/chapter-1-registrars-of-voters/section-ii-105-sale-of-voter-registration-lists) (search summary)
- **TX.** The SOS furnishes statewide list data under Tex. Elec. Code § 18.066 to any requester who signs a notarized non-commercial-use affidavit. The data is furnished within 15 days. — [TX SOS Form 24-1, prescribed 10/2026](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/forms/pol-sub/24-1f.pdf)
- **WV.** Under W. Va. Code § 3-2-30, the SOS sells statewide lists to any purchaser who certifies non-commercial use. County clerks provide lists "for noncommercial purposes." — [WV SOS request form Rev. 9/22/2026](https://sos.wv.gov/media/656/download); [W. Va. Code § 3-2-30](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/3-2-30/)
- **OK.** Effective 1 Nov 2025, under 26 O.S. § 7-103.2, access to the voter registration list is limited to: (a) "bona fide Oklahoma residents who are U.S. citizens" seeking it for personal use; (b) official representatives of recognized parties; (c) candidates for Oklahoma office; and (d) others authorized by law. Access is through a free one-year "full access" account on the OK Election Data Warehouse (EDW). Anyone else gets "basic" access, which includes absentee-request lists and deleted-voter lists but not the voter registration list. — [OK SEB Voter Registration List page (modified 24 Nov 2025)](https://oklahoma.gov/elections/candidates/voter-registration-list.html); [EDW Information Packet 8/2025](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/elections/ok-election-data-warehouse/readme-vrlist.pdf)
- **SC.** "Any registered voter in South Carolina may purchase a list of voters." — [SEC Attachment to House Oversight Committee, May 2017](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/CommitteeInfo/HouseLegislativeOversightCommittee/AgencyWebpages/ElectionCommission/Voter%20Information%20Available%20for%20Sale.pdf) **[pre-2024, possibly outdated]**. In 2024 the SEC denied an out-of-state organization's request under S.C. Code § 7-3-20(D)(13), which requires the requester to be a qualified SC voter. — [Democracy Docket case page](https://www.democracydocket.com/cases/south-carolina-voter-purge-pilf/); [D.S.C. order 18 Sep 2024](https://www.democracydocket.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/382024-09-18-Order-granting-PILFs-motion-for-summary-judgment.pdf)
- **TN.** T.C.A. § 2-2-138: the list and any compiled voter history "shall be available to any person who certifies on a form provided by the state election commission that such list will be used for political purposes." — [Justia, TCA 2-2-138 (2021)](https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/2021/title-2/chapter-2/part-1/section-2-2-138/) **[2021 text, check for amendments]**. Sales are handled by county election commissions, e.g. Rutherford County. — [Rutherford County Voter Data](https://rutherfordcountytn.gov/voter-data). One search summary said "Voter lists are only available to residents of Tennessee," but the primary source for that claim was not identified (see Gaps).
- **VA.** Va. Code § 24.2-405(A): the Department of Elections provides lists only to candidates ("to further their candidacy"), party committees and officials ("for political purposes only"), qualifying PACs, incumbent officeholders (for constituent reporting), nonprofits promoting voter participation and registration, commissioners of the revenue and treasurers (tax purposes), and courts (jury selection, free). "The lists shall be furnished to no one else and used for no other purpose." — [Va. Code § 24.2-405](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/24.2-405). Members of the public who want to promote participation can receive only a "List of Those Who Voted." — [VA ELECT Client Services](https://www.elections.virginia.gov/candidatepac-info/client-services) (search summary; page returned 403)
- **KY.** Under KRS 117.025(3)(h), the State Board furnishes precinct lists to statutorily qualified recipients and, at its discretion, to others. 31 KAR 3:010 § 5 (2021 amendment) bars that discretion from extending to requests "known to come from outside of the United States" or likely to result in data "leaving the United States." Requests are made on form SBE-84. — [31 KAR 3:010 (2021 amended text)](https://elect.ky.gov/Resources/Documents/31%20KAR%203.010.pdf) **[2021 text; further amended Oct 2025, details not found]**. The EAC classes KY as "Mixed." — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)

### Inferences
- Only NC combines free, anonymous, account-free, weekly bulk download of every registrant with a linked participation file. MS now comes close (free and weekly, but needs an email sign-up). FL is free but needs a request each month. OK is free but, since Nov 2025, limited to residents and political actors.
- Restricting access to in-state residents (SC, OK, and possibly TN) is being tested under the NVRA public-disclosure provision (see Q7). This is directly relevant to any Korean design that would limit access by nationality or residence.

### Gaps
- The full NCSL table could not be retrieved (403).
- TN: whether the statewide list is sold by the Division of Elections, or only by county commissions, was not found. The source of the "residents only" statement was not found.
- KY: the full list of "statutorily qualified" recipients in KRS 117.025 was not retrieved, and the content of the Oct 2025 amendment to 31 KAR 3:010 was not found.
- AL: governor's approval of HB67 was not verified.

## Q2. Which fields are included, and which are withheld?

### Takeaway
Southern files are among the richest in the US. **Race** is published in NC, FL, GA, LA and SC (Texas publishes only a "Hispanic surname" flag). **Full date of birth** is released in TX, OK, SC and FL, and optionally in WV. **Birth year or age only** in NC, GA, VA, KY and LA. **No age at all** in MS. Party appears everywhere except MS (party appears only in its primary history), and VA, which has no party registration (VA's registered-voter list was not found to carry a party field). SSNs and driver-licence numbers are withheld everywhere. Phone and email are withheld in most states, but **NC publishes phone numbers**, and FL has historically published phone and email.

### Cited Findings
- **NC (file layout updated 02/02/2026).** Fields: county; voter_reg_num; NCID; last/first/middle name and suffix; status code and description (A active, I inactive, R removed, D denied, S temporary); status reason; residential street address, city, state, ZIP; mailing address lines 1–4, city, state, ZIP; **full_phone_number**; confidential_ind; registr_dt; **race_code** (A, B, I, M, O, P, U, W); **ethnic_code** (HL, NL, UN); party_cd; gender_code; **birth_year**; **age_at_year_end** (replaced "birth_age" on 09/02/2022); birth_state; drivers_lic (Y/N); and, new from 02/01/2026, ssn (Y/N, "last four of SSN"), no_dl_ssn_chkbx (Y/N) and hava_id_req (Y/N). It also carries precinct, municipality, ward, congressional, superior court, judicial, NC Senate, NC House, county commissioner, township, school, fire, water, sewer, sanitation, rescue, municipal, prosecutorial and VTD district codes. Removed registrations are omitted once the last vote was more than 10 years ago. Names shown with "#" or registration dates shown as "xx/xx/xxxx" are masked while under county review "to prevent unintentional release of confidential or incorrect information." — [layout_ncvoter.txt](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/data/layout_ncvoter.txt)
- **NC (withheld).** "Voter birthdate, Social Security number, and driver's license number are confidential under state law and are not included." — [NCSBE](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-registration-data). By statute, confidential items are: full or partial SSN; dates of birth; the public agency where the voter registered; email addresses; driver's licence numbers; and photographs or copies of ID. Signatures "may be viewed by the public but may not be copied or traced." Lists "shall not contain a voter's date of birth," but ages may be shown instead. — [G.S. 163-82.10(a1),(a2),(c)](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10.html); [G.S. 163-82.10B](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/PDF/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10B.pdf)
- **TX (Form 24-1, 10/2026).** Fields: county, precinct, VUID, last/first/middle/**former last name**, suffix, gender, **DOB (8 digits)**, residence address components, mailing address, effective date of registration, status (Active, Suspense, Cancelled) and Hispanic surname flag ("not an indication of ethnicity"). — [TX SOS Form 24-1](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/forms/pol-sub/24-1f.pdf). Withheld: SSN, DL number, personal ID card number, and interest in serving as an election judge. Phone, SSN, DL, DOB and protected persons' residential addresses "cannot be posted online." — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf) **[undated]**
- **OK (EDW packet 8/2025).** Fields: voter ID, name, **date of birth**, precinct, registration address, mailing address, political affiliation, status (A/I), municipality, school district, technology center district, last 10 election dates and voting methods, county, and voter ID card status (undeliverable). DL and SSN "are never released." Phone and email "are not part of a voter's registration record." — [OK EDW Information Packet](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/elections/ok-election-data-warehouse/readme-vrlist.pdf)
- **SC.** Fields: county code, registration number, full name, residence and mailing address, gender, **race**, registration date, **date of birth**, date last voted, participation in the last 2 general elections and the last 2 Democratic and Republican primaries, precinct and all districts, and voter status. — [SEC 2017 attachment](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/CommitteeInfo/HouseLegislativeOversightCommittee/AgencyWebpages/ElectionCommission/Voter%20Information%20Available%20for%20Sale.pdf) **[pre-2024]**. The EAC adds phone number as included and SSN as withheld. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **FL.** The extract layout includes race codes (including "Hispanic" as a race category, Multi-racial and Unknown) and party codes. — [FL extract layout rev. 2025-05-16](https://files.floridados.gov/media/708728/final-voter-extract-disk-file-layout-rev-20250516.pdf) (search summary; file returned 403). The Division of Elections has stated that "with few exceptions, all voter registration information is public record including your name, address, date of birth, party affiliation, phone number and email address." — quoted in [News4Jax 2020](https://news4jax.com/news/2020/10/21/registered-to-vote-in-florida-some-of-your-data-is-public) **[pre-2024]**. The Dec 2022 layout flags daytime phone, phone extension and email as "protected" fields (positions 36–38). — [FL layout 2022-12](https://files.floridados.gov/media/706185/voter-extract-disk-file-layout-202212.pdf) (search summary; meaning of the flag not confirmed). Exempt under § 97.0585: SSN, DL/ID number, signature, where the person registered or updated, and declinations. — [Fla. Stat. 97.0585 (2024)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/statutes/2024/97.0585); [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **GA.** The SOS order form says voter lists "do not include telephone numbers, Date of Birth, full or partial SSN#, or DL#." — [GA SOS order form](https://sos.ga.gov/voterlistorderform031822) (search summary). The EAC says the file includes "all information except what is listed as confidential, including year of birth," and withholds month and day of birth, SSN, email, DL and the location of registration. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf). Demos (2023) scored Georgia's purchasable file 100% for data transparency. — [Demos GA excerpt](https://www.demos.org/sites/default/files/2023-11/Georgia%20-%20Excerpt%20-%20Protecting%20Voter%20Registration.pdf) **[pre-2024]**
- **LA.** Text-format list content: parish, name, ward, precinct, party, residence and mailing addresses, sex, **race**, **age**, status, registration date, registration number, last 20 dates voted, and all districts. — [LAC 31:II.105](https://d1tvxue8o4w0fl.cloudfront.net/regulation/louisiana-administrative-code/title-31-elections/part-ii-voter-registration/chapter-1-registrars-of-voters/section-ii-105-sale-of-voter-registration-lists) (search summary). The SOS's 2024 charges sheet also lists **phone number** and last vote date. — [LA SOS VoterListChargesAndInfo.pdf](https://www.sos.la.gov/ElectionsAndVoting/PublishedDocuments/VoterListChargesAndInfo.pdf) (search summary; URL now 404). Withheld: SSN, DL, day and month of birth, mother's maiden name, email, SMS number, the fact that a voter is entitled to assistance, declinations, and the agency or office of registration. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **MS (2025 program).** Registration file: mapping value (ID), first, middle and suffix name (the program text omits last name for this file but lists it for the history file), residential address components, county, precinct code and name, mailing address, effective registration date, voter status, date last voted, and all districts. History file: ID, name, election name, election type, political party. No DOB, gender or race field is listed. — [MS SOS program PDF](https://www.sos.ms.gov/content/documents/about_us/2025%20Voter%20File%20Weekly%20Distribution%20Program.pdf). Withheld: SSN, DOB, age, DL, phone, email, fax, county voter number, custom fields, last jury date and registration source. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **VA.** Lists contain "name, address, year of birth, gender and all election districts." Withheld: any part of SSN, addresses of protected persons, declinations, the registration agency, and birth day and month. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf). Where an individual has provided a PO box, the list shows it "in lieu of the residence street address" (§ 24.2-405(D)). SSN is excluded except for jury, tax and other-state election uses. — [Va. Code § 24.2-405](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/24.2-405)
- **KY.** Precinct lists contain "name, address, age code, party, gender, zip code, and five (5) year voting history." — [31 KAR 3:010 § 1(3),(9)](https://elect.ky.gov/Resources/Documents/31%20KAR%203.010.pdf) **[2021 text]**. SSN is redacted. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **WV (form Rev. 9/22/2026).** Default fields: name, residence address and county, party, status, registration date. Optional add-ons: **birthdate**, mailing address, precinct, and congressional, state senate, delegate and magisterial districts. — [WV SOS form](https://sos.wv.gov/media/656/download). Withheld: phone, email, SSN, DL. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)
- **AL (HB67, from 1 June 2026).** May not be disseminated: SSN, DL number, email, telephone numbers (unless a voter registering or updating on or after 1 June 2026 opts in), and voter information protected by § 17-4-33(b)(1). — [AL HB67 enr.](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB67-enr.pdf). Earlier practice withheld "SSN, month and day of birth" (year of birth released). — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf) **[undated]**
- **AR.** Certain fields are "never released," per Ark. Const. Amend. 51 § 8(e). — [AR SOS form](https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/elections/Data%20Request%20Form.pdf) (search summary). The EAC lists the contents as "names, addresses and precinct numbers," with withheld fields "not specified." — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)

### Inferences
- Race appears in the files of NC, FL, GA, LA and SC, and in AL if AL releases "all voter registration information except confidential." These are states whose registration forms ask for race, a legacy of Voting Rights Act-era monitoring. This is background context, not sourced in this research.
- NC sits in the middle on identifiers: it withholds DOB but publishes birth year, age, birth state, gender, race, ethnicity and phone. Combined with name and address, that is enough to re-identify people, as academic work using NC data shows (Q6). TX and OK publish full DOB, which NC does not.
- The NC Feb 2026 layout adds flags showing whether a record holds DL or SSN-4 and whether HAVA ID is required. These flags appear to respond to the HAVA "Registration Repair" dispute (Q7). They make public which voters are missing ID numbers.

### Gaps
- AR field list, GA's full current field list, FL's current (2025) field list and the meaning of FL's "protected" flag, and AL's actual current fields could not be confirmed from primary pages (403 or 404).
- Whether VA's list includes party: VA has no party registration, and no source for a party field was found.

## Q3. Is voter participation history included (which elections, voting method)? Absentee/mail-ballot request lists?

### Takeaway
Every state in the group makes some participation history available. Several publish **voting method** by election: NC (very granular, 10 years), TX (in person early, election day, absentee accepted or rejected, provisional), OK (method for regular federal and state elections only, by statute), and FL (10 years). Primary party ballot history is common: NC, MS, SC, TX, WV. **Pre-election absentee request and return lists** are public in NC, OK, SC (from 9 a.m. the day before the election), WV (for sale) and VA (List of Those Who Voted). FL keeps vote-by-mail request data confidential except to specified political users.

### Cited Findings
- **NC history file (ncvhis).** Contains county, voter_reg_num, election label and description, **voting_method**, **voted_party_cd and voted_party_desc** (the party ballot in primaries), precinct and VTD at the time of the election, NCID, and voted county. It has no names, is linkable to the voter file by NCID, and is "limited to election dates within the last 10 years." — [layout_ncvhis.txt](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/data/layout_ncvhis.txt). The NCSBE says the files contain "a data entry for each election in which a voter participated in the past 10 years." Statewide and county ZIPs are updated weekly, and finalization can take weeks after an election. — [NCSBE Voter History Data](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-history-data)
- **NC voting-method values.** These were observed by this researcher on 2026-11-12 by tallying the public Tyrrell County history file (ncvhis89, covering elections 11/07/2017–03/03/2026): ELECTION DAY IN-PERSON, ABSENTEE ONESTOP, EARLY VOTING IN-PERSON, ABSENTEE BY MAIL, ABSENTEE CURBSIDE, EARLY VOTING CURBSIDE, ELECTION DAY CURBSIDE, PROVISIONAL, TRANSFER. — [ncvhis89.zip](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/data/ncvhis89.zip) (the downloaded file was deleted after counting).
- **NC (statute).** Public lists may include "voter history including primary, general, and special districts." — [G.S. 163-82.10(c)](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10.html). The NCSBE also publishes **Election Day and 1 January snapshot files**, which keep removed voters for 10 years. — [NCSBE](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-registration-data)
- **NC absentee files.** The NCSBE publishes statewide and per-county absentee files with columns such as abs_req_type (e.g., MAIL) and ballot_rtn_status (e.g., ACCEPTED, ACCEPTED - CURED). — [NCSBE "How to Work with Our Data"](https://www.ncsbe.gov/about-elections/county-boards-elections/county-resources/county-board-kit/how-work-our-data); [NCSBE Outstanding Ballots report](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/absentee-and-provisional-data/outstanding-ballots-data-report-summary). One county page (2022) says detailed absentee-by-mail information "remains confidential" until the ballot is returned or the polls open. — [Greene County NC](https://greenecountync.gov/early-voting-turnout/) **[pre-2024]**
- **TX.** History is selectable for any election from 1996 to the present. The history file carries election date, type (GE, CP, RU, SE, LO, PO, LR), election party, and voting method: EV (early in person), ED (election day), AV, AX and AB (absentee accepted, rejected, received), PB and PX (provisional accepted, rejected). — [TX Form 24-1](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/forms/pol-sub/24-1f.pdf)
- **OK.** The registration file has the last 10 election dates and methods. The history file holds voter ID plus election dates and methods. By law, method is public only "for a regularly scheduled federal or state election OR a statewide special election for a state question"; otherwise it is shown as ***. Absentee ballot request lists for each election and a 24-month deleted-voter list are available even with basic access. — [OK EDW packet](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/elections/ok-election-data-warehouse/readme-vrlist.pdf); [26 O.S. § 7-138 via OK SEB](https://oklahoma.gov/elections/candidates/voter-registration-list.html)
- **FL.** The extract contains "the prior 10 years of official voting history for federal and state elections and the prior 10 years of unofficial voting history for local elections," with history codes. — [FL extract layout 2025](https://files.floridados.gov/media/708728/final-voter-extract-disk-file-layout-rev-20250516.pdf) (search summary). Vote-by-mail request information is confidential under § 101.62(2), F.S., apart from authorized recipients. — [Brevard SOE "Who can acquire mail ballot data?"](https://votebrevard.gov/Statistics-and-Data/Request-Mail-Ballot-Data) (search summary)
- **SC.** Lists contain the last 2 general elections and the last 2 Democratic and Republican primaries. Under § 7-15-330, from 9:00 a.m. on the day before any election, data on every voter in the absentee process is public: name, address, absentee address, application request, issue and return dates, ballot delivery and return method, who requested the ballot, who returned it (by name), party voted in the primary, and UOCAVA reason. — [SEC 2017 attachment](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/CommitteeInfo/HouseLegislativeOversightCommittee/AgencyWebpages/ElectionCommission/Voter%20Information%20Available%20for%20Sale.pdf) **[pre-2024]**
- **MS.** The weekly history file has one row per election voted, with election name, election type and political party. — [MS SOS program](https://www.sos.ms.gov/content/documents/about_us/2025%20Voter%20File%20Weekly%20Distribution%20Program.pdf)
- **WV.** For sale: Master Voter History List Export ($500), Statewide Early Voters List ($200), Statewide Absentee Requests List ($200) and Statewide Absentee Received List ($200). The subscription updates the absentee lists daily from 30 days before an election until 10 days after it. — [WV SOS form](https://sos.wv.gov/media/656/download)
- **AL.** From 1 June 2026, applicants receive "election history information for the last 10 election cycles in which a voter participated." — [AL HB67](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB67-enr.pdf)
- **AR.** The VH file has voter ID and vote history "for all Federal elections from 2008," without names. — [AR SOS form](https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/elections/Data%20Request%20Form.pdf) (search summary)
- **LA.** The text list includes "last 20 dates voted." — [LAC 31:II.105](https://d1tvxue8o4w0fl.cloudfront.net/regulation/louisiana-administrative-code/title-31-elections/part-ii-voter-registration/chapter-1-registrars-of-voters/section-ii-105-sale-of-voter-registration-lists) (search summary)
- **KY.** Five-year voting history appears in precinct lists. — [31 KAR 3:010](https://elect.ky.gov/Resources/Documents/31%20KAR%203.010.pdf)
- **TN.** "The list and any voter history, if compiled" is available. Rutherford County sells history on CD covering 1 to 10 years back. — [TCA 2-2-138](https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/2021/title-2/chapter-2/part-1/section-2-2-138/); [Rutherford County](https://rutherfordcountytn.gov/voter-data)

This comes from a search summary of a GA–DOJ letter; the letter itself was not opened. — [GA letter to DOJ, 8 Dec 2025 (hosted by Daily Signal)](https://www.dailysignal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Ltr-to-H-Dhillon-12.8.25.pdf)
- **VA.** "Voting history is also available to candidates, parties, etc. for political purposes." — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf). The public can obtain only the List of Those Who Voted. — [VA ELECT](https://www.elections.virginia.gov/candidatepac-info/client-services) (search summary)

### Inferences
- In NC, TX and OK, the published "voting method" field lets any user see, for each named voter, whether they voted early, by mail, curbside or provisionally, but not how they voted. Ballot secrecy is preserved, but participation and method are fully public. Primary-party-ballot fields reveal which party's primary a voter chose.

### Gaps
- GA's voter-history file format and price, and LA's and AR's voting-method detail, were not found.

## Q4. Use restrictions, affidavits, penalties, statute citations

### Takeaway
The dominant restriction in the region is **"no commercial use."** It appears in TX (notarized affidavit; Class A misdemeanor), WV (no commercial or charitable solicitation, no resale; signed certification), AL (from 2026, unlawful to sell, publish or use for commercial purposes), KY (regulation defining commercial use; scholarly, journalistic and political uses exempt) and SC (general § 30-2-50 ban on commercial solicitation; misdemeanor). **Purpose-limited** regimes: TN (political purposes; false certification is a Class B misdemeanor) and VA (only listed purposes; ban on posting a searchable list online). **NC and AR have no statutory use restriction**; AR states this explicitly.

### Cited Findings
- **TX.** Requesters swear the information "will not be used to advertise or promote commercial products or services" before a notary. Under Tex. Elec. Code § 18.067, knowing commercial use of § 18.066 information is a Class A misdemeanor. — [TX Form 24-1](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/forms/pol-sub/24-1f.pdf)
- **WV.** § 3-2-30(f): "No voter registration lists or data files ... may be used for commercial or charitable solicitations or advertising, sold or reproduced for resale." Purchasers sign a certification. — [WV SOS form](https://sos.wv.gov/media/656/download)
- **AL (HB67, effective 1 June 2026).** "It shall be unlawful to sell or publish any portion of a voter list or use voter data ... for commercial purposes." Use "solely for electioneering or for other permitted political campaign or election purposes under the Fair Campaign Practices Act does not constitute commercial use." — [AL HB67](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB67-enr.pdf)
- **KY.** Commercial use under KRS 117.025(3)(h) means use "for profit, the solicitation of donations, or for the sale or advertisement of any good, or service, or publication," or onward transfer for those purposes. Exceptions: "scholarly, journalistic, political (including political fund raising), or governmental purposes," and news-media use. — [31 KAR 3:010 §§ 3–4](https://elect.ky.gov/Resources/Documents/31%20KAR%203.010.pdf)
- **SC.** Using public records for commercial solicitation is prohibited under § 30-2-50. A knowing violation is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to $500, imprisonment of up to 1 year, or both. — [SEC 2017 attachment](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/CommitteeInfo/HouseLegislativeOversightCommittee/AgencyWebpages/ElectionCommission/Voter%20Information%20Available%20for%20Sale.pdf) **[pre-2024]**
- **TN.** Requesters certify use "for political purposes." A false certification is a Class B misdemeanor punishable by a $500 fine. County vendors may use data only to serve the commission. — [TCA 2-2-138](https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/2021/title-2/chapter-2/part-1/section-2-2-138/) **[2021 text]**
- **VA.** Lists are to be "furnished to no one else and used for no other purpose." Recipients may not publish list information "on the Internet as a searchable list or database" or give it to a third party for that purpose (§ 24.2-405(E)). — [Va. Code § 24.2-405](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/24.2-405). Recipients sign a Statement of Responsibility. — [VA ELECT Statement of Responsibility](https://elections.es.virginia.gov/media/formswarehouse/records-access-and-retention/lists/Statement-of-Responsibility-Voter-Lists.pdf). A credit card used for payment must be in the name of a person or organization authorized under § 24.2-405. — [VA ELECT](https://www.elections.virginia.gov/candidatepac-info/client-services) (search summary)
- **AR.** "Currently there are no state laws that place restrictions on the use of data that we release." — [AR SOS form](https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/elections/Data%20Request%20Form.pdf) (search summary)
- **NC.** No statutory limit on use of public lists was found beyond the confidentiality rules and the no-copying rule for signatures. — [G.S. 163-82.10](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10.html)
- **OK.** No penalty for misuse is mentioned on the SEB page. Access to the list is limited by requester category (26 O.S. § 7-103.2). — [OK SEB](https://oklahoma.gov/elections/candidates/voter-registration-list.html)
- **EAC (general).** "Even the least restrictive states prohibit the commercial use of voter file information." Once data leaves the source, "there are no technical limitations on how the data is reused, redistributed, or consolidated." — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf)

### Inferences
- In NC and AR, the absence of a use restriction is unusual. It means voter-challenge operations, commercial look-up sites and researchers all draw on the same file, and the state has no legal lever over downstream use.
- VA's ban on publishing a searchable online database is the regional model for blocking "look up your neighbour" sites, while keeping the data available to campaigns.

### Gaps
- Use restrictions in FL, GA, LA and MS were not found in primary sources. The EAC classes FL, GA, LA and MS as "Open," which suggests only the general commercial-use limits, if any.

## Q6. Protections for at-risk voters (address confidentiality, judges, police) and opt-outs; privacy criticism

### Takeaway
Protections are mostly **Address Confidentiality Program (ACP) and protective-order based**, and narrow. NC, OK, VA, TX and AL have documented exclusions. No state here was found to give a general opt-out from the public list. Phone and email are the only fields voters can sometimes control (FL voluntarily; AL opt-in from 2026). Judges and police: an NC bill (H826, 2023–24) was introduced but its enactment was not verified. Florida's ch. 119 exemptions were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- **NC.** A voter who submits a protective order or a current ACP card, plus a signed statement, has the address kept confidential while the order or participation lasts. The address "is a public record but shall be kept confidential." Name, precinct and other registration data stay public. Law enforcement and persons named in a court order may inspect the address, and it is not given to the jury commission. — [G.S. 163-82.10(e)](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10.html). The ACP (Chapter 15C, created 2002) covers victims of domestic violence, sexual offenses and stalking, who use an Attorney General substitute address. — [G.S. 15C-8 (2011)](https://law.justia.com/codes/north-carolina/2011/chapter15c/section15c-8/) **[pre-2024]**. The public file carries a "confidential_ind" flag. — [layout](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/data/layout_ncvoter.txt)
- **NC (judges and police).** H826 (2023–2024), "Protect Law Enforcement/Judges Personal Info," was introduced. Enactment was not verified. — [LegiScan H826](https://legiscan.com/NC/text/H826/id/2783389)
- **OK.** Address information for "Restricted Records" participants and all registration information for ACP participants are excluded from the list (26 O.S. § 4-115.2). Method of voting is suppressed for certain elections (§ 7-138). — [OK EDW packet](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/elections/ok-election-data-warehouse/readme-vrlist.pdf)
- **VA.** "Addresses of protected persons" are withheld. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf). Where a PO box is provided, it is shown instead of the street address. — [Va. Code § 24.2-405(D)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/24.2-405)
- **TX.** The residential address of "protected persons" cannot be posted online. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf) **[undated]**
- **AL.** Voter information protected by § 17-4-33(b)(1) may not be disseminated. Telephone numbers are released only for voters who opt in after 1 June 2026 (a checkbox on the registration form). — [AL HB67](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB67-enr.pdf)
- **FL.** Including phone or email is voluntary, and voters can remove them. — [ClickOrlando, Oct 2020](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2020/10/22/registered-to-vote-in-florida-some-of-your-data-is-public) **[pre-2024]**. A 2021 bill (CS/HB 155) described DOB, phone and email as "personal and sensitive data" that could be misused. Enactment was not confirmed. — [FL CS/HB 155 (2021)](https://flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2021/155/BillText/c1/PDF)
- **KY.** Foreign requesters are barred. — [31 KAR 3:010 § 5](https://elect.ky.gov/Resources/Documents/31%20KAR%203.010.pdf)
- **Privacy research using southern files.** Christen's NC Voter Registration dataset, built from downloads since 2011 and including race and ethnicity, is used for record-linkage research. — [Christen 2014](https://cs.anu.edu.au/people/Peter.Christen/publications/ncvoter-report-29june2014.pdf) **[pre-2024]**. A re-identification analysis using Robeson County (NC) and Travis County (TX) voter data found that name plus ZIP singles out many voters even with some redaction. — [SC Media brief](https://www.scworld.com/brief/public-voter-data-poses-privacy-risks-analysis-finds) (secondary report)
- **Leaks and "dark web" claims.** The EAC warns that reports of voter data on the dark web are often "false positives," because the data is publicly purchasable. — [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/voters/Available_Voter_File_Information.pdf). Example: 2018 reports of a hacker "selling" the GA voter list for $250, the same as the official price. — [Georgia Pol, Oct 2018](https://georgiapol.com/2018/10/16/hacker-selling-reported-ga-voter-list-for-250) **[pre-2024]**

### Inferences
- In the US South, privacy protection works by **carving individuals out** (ACP and protective orders) and **removing fields** (DOB in NC, phone and email in AL). It does not work through a general right to opt out of the public list. For Korea, this means "publish, with exclusions" models depend heavily on the size of the protected-person programme.

### Gaps
- Florida's § 119.071 home-address exemptions for judges, police and others, and how they appear in the extract, were not verified (FL pages returned 403).
- Formal ACP exclusion rules for GA, SC, TN, LA, MS, AR, KY and WV were not found.
- No documented doxxing incident using a southern state voter file in 2020–2026 was found in this research.

## Q7. Significant changes and controversies 2020–2026, including responses to the 2025–2026 DOJ demands for full voter rolls

### Takeaway
(1) **Georgia**: SB 202 (2021) and SB 189 (2024) enabled mass voter challenges built on the purchasable list and tools such as EagleAI. Tens of thousands were challenged, few were removed. (2) **North Carolina**: the public file powered the Griffin challenge to more than 60,000 ballots (2024–25), and later the DOJ's HAVA suit and the Sept 2025 consent order ("Registration Repair"), with a reported clause to give the DOJ the full file if requested. (3) **Access rules shifted both ways**: OK restricted access to residents (Nov 2025), while MS (free weekly, 2025), VA (90% price cut, 2025) and AL (price cap, 2026) widened it. (4) **DOJ demands, 2025–2026**: TX, FL, LA, MS, AR, SC and TN provided data; OK settled (Mar 2026); NC settled within its HAVA case; GA, KY, VA and WV refused, were sued, and won dismissals (DOJ appealing, per NBC); AL's status is unclear.

### Cited Findings
- **GA mass challenges.** SB 189 (effective 1 July 2024) "establishes the types of evidence needed to challenge a voter's status," building on SB 202 (2021). Critics say it legitimizes EagleAI. — [GPB, Jul 2024](https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/07/08/groups-readying-mass-voter-challenges-in-georgias-2024-elections-theres-app-for); [NPR, Jun 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/06/04/nx-s1-4991945/voter-registration-mass-challenges-georgia). An AP tally found more than 63,000 voters challenged after 1 July 2024, but few removed. — [Fox 5 Atlanta, Oct 2024](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/republicans-challenge-georgia-voters-removal-rolls). This built on about 250,000 challenges in 2020 and more than 100,000 afterwards. — [GPB](https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/07/08/groups-readying-mass-voter-challenges-in-georgias-2024-elections-theres-app-for). The Cobb board dismissed EagleAI-based challenges to 2,472 voters. — [AJC](https://www.ajc.com/politics/cobb-elections-board-rejects-eagleai-based-voter-challenges/OMPU5WSC4NHPXANMECH45ZBRE4)
- **NC Griffin challenge (2024–25).** For the "incomplete registration" and "never resident" categories, the NC GOP hired a consultant "to review and compile publicly available State Board of Elections data." — [Democracy NC](https://democracync.org/nc-state-supreme-court-voter-challenge/). Myers II approved a settlement on 8 Sep 2025 memorializing the "Registration Repair Project." The settlement reportedly provides that the state will turn over an electronic copy of its voter file, including names, birth dates, addresses and partial DL or SSN, if requested, handled under the Privacy Act. — [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/north-carolina-agrees-to-turn-over-voter-records-in-settlement/) (search summary; page returned 403). The State Board denied this gives "unfettered access"; Democrats sought to reopen the settlement, and the DOJ opposed. — [Carolina Journal](https://www.carolinajournal.com/elections-board-rebuts-charge-of-feds-unfettered-access-to-nc-data/); [Carolina Journal](https://www.carolinajournal.com/justice-department-opposes-reopening-settlement-of-nc-election-dispute/). NBC lists NC as "legal settlement." — [NBC tracker, updated 2 Nov 2026](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/tracking-dojs-effort-get-us-voter-registration-data-rcna331509)

### Inferences

## Q8. North Carolina (headline example), consolidated profile

### Takeaway
NC is the most open voter-list regime in the region and among the most open in the US. Anyone, with no account, fee or purpose statement, can download weekly every registrant's name, residential and mailing address, phone, race, ethnicity, gender, birth year, age, birth state, party, registration date, status, every district, and ID-number flags. A linked file gives 10 years of participation by election, with voting method and primary party ballot. Full DOB, SSN, DL, email, registration agency and photos are withheld by G.S. 163-82.10(a1) and 163-82.10B. ACP and protective-order voters' addresses are kept confidential (163-82.10(e)). There is no statutory use restriction. The file underpinned the 2024–25 Griffin mass ballot challenge and is widely used in research.

### Cited Findings
- **Distribution.** Statewide and 100 county ZIPs, tab-delimited, updated weekly on Sunday evenings. Linked to history by NCID or by county plus registration number. Election Day and 1 January snapshots are kept, with removed voters retained for 10 years. — [NCSBE Voter Registration Data](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-registration-data)
- **Exact fields** (layout from 01 Feb 2026): see Q2. These include full_phone_number, race_code, ethnic_code, party_cd, gender_code, birth_year, age_at_year_end, birth_state, registr_dt, status and reason, confidential_ind, drivers_lic (Y/N), ssn (Y/N), hava_id_req, and more than 20 district fields. — [layout_ncvoter.txt](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/data/layout_ncvoter.txt)
- **History fields and voting methods**: see Q3. Ten years; election label; voting_method (election day in person, absentee one-stop, early voting in person, absentee by mail, curbside variants, provisional, transfer); voted party. — [layout_ncvhis.txt](https://s3.amazonaws.com/dl.ncsbe.gov/data/layout_ncvhis.txt); [NCSBE Voter History](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-history-data)
- **Aggregate products.** Voter registration stats and history stats by county, precinct, method, party, race, ethnicity, sex and age, from the 2000 general through the 3 Mar 2026 primary, with "no individual voter records." — [NCSBE Voter History](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-history-data)
- **Absentee files.** Statewide and county absentee files with request type and return status. — [NCSBE data guide](https://www.ncsbe.gov/about-elections/county-boards-elections/county-resources/county-board-kit/how-work-our-data)
- **Legal basis.** G.S. 163-82.10: (a) the State system is the official list; (a1) lists the confidential items (SSN, DOB, agency, email, DL, photos and ID copies), which "shall not be considered public records," while cumulative data may be released if no individual can be identified; (a2) signatures may be viewed but not copied; (c) county boards must provide lists on request at actual cost (media charge no more than $25), lists may include voter history, and "No list ... shall contain a voter's date of birth" (ages allowed); (d) free lists to party chairs; (e) protective-order and ACP address confidentiality. — [G.S. 163-82.10](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10.html). G.S. 163-82.10B requires boards to keep DOB confidential, with exceptions for candidates, challenged voters, written consent and jury commissions. — [G.S. 163-82.10B](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/PDF/BySection/Chapter_163/GS_163-82.10B.pdf). The general public records law (G.S. Chapter 132) is the backdrop: (a1) items are excluded "as public records." The text of G.S. 132-1 was not separately fetched.
- **Confidentiality programme.** ACP (G.S. Chapter 15C, 2002) for victims of domestic violence, sexual offenses and stalking. — [G.S. 15C-8](https://law.justia.com/codes/north-carolina/2011/chapter15c/section15c-8/) **[pre-2024]**
- **Criticism, misuse and litigation.** The Griffin challenge was built on public SBE data (Democracy NC, WRAL, ProPublica, Forward Justice; see Q7). The DOJ HAVA suit and 2025 consent order reportedly include a clause for full-file transfer, and Democrats sought to reopen it (Courthouse News, Carolina Journal; see Q7). NC data is used in academic record-linkage and re-identification studies (Christen; Kenney via SC Media; see Q6).

### Inferences
- For Korea, NC is the upper bound of "publish the roll." Its model rests on (a) withholding exact DOB and national ID numbers, (b) carving out ACP participants, and (c) leaving downstream use unregulated. In 2024–25 the third choice let a candidate's party mine the file to target a demographically skewed set of voters.

### Gaps
- The text of G.S. 132-1 and any 2024–2026 NC session law adding judge or police address confidentiality to voter records were not found (H826 status unverified).
